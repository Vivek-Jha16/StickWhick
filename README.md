# 🎨 StickWhick

**Turn Any Photo Into a Premium Sticker**

StickWhick is a modern AI-powered sticker maker that removes the background from your photos directly inside your browser and creates beautiful die-cut PNG stickers.

> ⚡ No signup • No uploads • Everything runs locally in your browser.

---

# ✨ Features

- 📸 Upload images from your device
- 📷 Capture photos using your camera
- 🤖 AI-powered background removal
- ✂️ Automatic sticker cutout
- 🤍 Adjustable sticker border
- 🎨 Multiple border colors
- 🌟 Smooth edge adjustment
- 🎭 3D sticker animation
- 📱 Fully mobile responsive
- 🔒 Privacy-first (images never leave your device)
- ⬇️ Download transparent PNG stickers

# 📖 How to Use

1. Open StickWhick.
2. Upload a photo or use your camera.
3. Wait for AI to remove the background.
4. Customize the sticker border.
5. Download your transparent PNG sticker.
